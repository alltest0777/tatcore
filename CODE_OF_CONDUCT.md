# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or personal characteristics.

As members, contributors, and maintainers of the TatCoin project, we pledge to foster an open, collaborative, and harassment-free environment.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Helping other community members.
- Focusing on what is best for the project.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Showing empathy toward others.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Threats or abusive language.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all official TatCoin spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories
- Discussions
- Pull Requests
- Issue tracker
- Documentation
- Community communication channels
- Events organized by the project

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions that violate these guidelines.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from participating in the project.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers.

Reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed fairly.
